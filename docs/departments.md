# For departments

The units can be used as course content: for example as the practical
component of a numerical analysis course, or in a research methods course for
final-year and MSc students. The material is licensed under CC BY 4.0, so you
may adapt it, translate it and include it in your own course notes.

## A six-week practical with unit A1

One session of two to three hours per week, in a computer laboratory or on
students' own laptops.

| Week | Topic | Tutorial sections | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Setting up; the initial value problem | 0, 1 | Python working; first report |
| 2 | Euler's method; measuring convergence order | 2, 3 | Euler passes all gates |
| 3 | Heun's method and classical RK4 | 4 | All three methods pass |
| 4 | Comparing methods by cost | 5 | Error–cost plot interpreted |
| 5 | Stability and stiffness | 6 | Stability limits explained |
| 6 | Project brief and report | 7, 8 | Short written report with the verification report attached |

**Assessment:** the unit's [marking rubric](units/ode-ivp/supervisors/rubric.md)
can be adapted for coursework; a short viva in week 6 confirms that each
student understands their own code.

**Requirements:** Python 3.10 or later with NumPy, SciPy and Matplotlib.
Internet access is needed only once, to install them; after that everything
runs offline. A laptop with 4 GB of memory is enough.

## Research methods and digital skills

The *ICT–STEM Transformation Strategy for Higher Education 2026–2036*, a policy
proposal submitted to the National Universities Commission in July 2026,
includes a research methods and digital skills programme for doctoral training,
and emphasises scientific computing and research integrity, including
reproducibility. Units like these are one practical way to teach those skills
now, at undergraduate and master's level. (verifiedmethods is independent: it
is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NUC.)

If your department would like to use the units in a course, or to discuss a
unit for a method your students use often, please get in touch (see
[About](about.md)).
